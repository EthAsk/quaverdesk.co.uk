Roadmap

What we're working on for Quaver Desk, and what's planned after that. Plans can change, and there are no dates here: a feature is ready when it's ready and tested. What's already been released is in the changelog.

Next

  • Call queues with hold music (Plus and Pro). Callers wait in a queue, hearing your hold music, until someone is free to answer, instead of going straight to voicemail.
  • Single sign-on with Microsoft and Google (Pro). Your team signs in with their work Microsoft 365 or Google account.
  • Minute bundles (hosted plans). Add extra UK call minutes when you need them, with clear charges if you go over your allowance.
  • Hosted plans for partners. IT firms and MSPs in the partner programme will be able to sell and look after hosted phone systems for their clients, as well as licences.

Later

  • Webhooks and Zapier (Plus and Pro). Send call events to your other systems.
  • AI search across all your calls (Pro). Find any call by what was said in it.
  • AI receptionist (Pro). Answers calls, finds out what the caller needs and puts them through to the right person.
  • HubSpot CRM integration (Pro). Calls and summaries logged against your contacts.
  • Live wallboard (Pro). A screen showing calls waiting, who's on a call and today's numbers.
  • Mobile apps for iPhone and Android.
  • AI without your own provider account (hosted plans). Use the AI features without setting up an account with an AI provider.
