Integration

A phone system that works with HubSpot

Quaver Desk connects to HubSpot CRM so your calls are on the contact record without anyone typing them up. The integration is part of the Pro plan and uses a private app token from your own HubSpot account.

What it does

  • Logs every call. Incoming and outgoing calls are logged against the HubSpot contact with that phone number: who took it, when, how long and how it ended. Calls between your own people are left out.
  • Adds the summary. When the AI summary, action items and sentiment are ready, they're added to the same HubSpot call.
  • Links the recording. A link to the recording or voicemail is added. People sign in to Quaver Desk to open it, so recordings never sit in HubSpot.
  • Shows who's calling. Incoming calls from a HubSpot contact show their name and company on the phone and in call history.
  • Click to call. Phone numbers in HubSpot open the Quaver Desk phone with the number filled in, ready to dial.
  • Makes contacts, if you want. Calls from numbers HubSpot doesn't know can create a contact, or be left alone. You choose.

Setting it up

  1. In HubSpot, create a private app with read and write access to contacts and calls.
  2. Paste its token into Admin → HubSpot in Quaver Desk and tick what you want logged.
  3. Press Test the connection.

The token is stored encrypted and never shown again. There is nothing to install in HubSpot, and it works with HubSpot's free CRM.

Other CRMs and tools

For anything else, Quaver Desk has webhooks, Zapier support and an API: send call events to your own systems, or read people, numbers and call history from them.
