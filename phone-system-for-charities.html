Guide

A phone system for charities and not-for-profits

Small organisations often have one phone number and staff or volunteers in different places. Quaver Desk lets everyone answer the same number from a browser or a desk phone, or have it ring their own mobile, without paying for a seat per person.

Why it suits small organisations

  • Reduced pricing for charities and not-for-profits. Get in touch and tell us about your organisation.
  • Free for up to 3 users, with no time limit. Paid plans are priced by team size, not per person, and no VAT is added.
  • Answer from anywhere. The phone in the browser needs nothing installed, and calls can ring someone's own mobile with "press 1 to accept", so they never end up in a personal voicemail.
  • Opening hours. Outside your hours, callers hear a message or leave voicemail, which arrives by email.
  • A menu such as "press 1 for bookings, 2 for advice" sends callers to the right people.

Don't want to run a server?

We can host it for you, normally from £49 a month, with up to 5 people, 2 numbers and 1,000 minutes of UK calls included, and updates and backups looked after. Ask about not-for-profit pricing.
