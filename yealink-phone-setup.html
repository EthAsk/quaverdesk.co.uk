Set up Yealink phones with Quaver Desk

Yealink T-series desk phones set themselves up on Quaver Desk. You add the phone by its MAC address, point it at your provisioning address once, and it downloads its account, busy lamp keys and settings, then checks for changes every day.

Which Yealink phones

The T-series desk phones (T3x, T4x and T5x, such as the T31P, T33G, T43U, T46U, T53 and T54W) on current firmware. Phones only connect encrypted, so very old firmware may need updating first. For cordless handsets, see Yealink DECT set-up.

Three steps

  1. Add the phone. In Quaver Desk go to Admin → Phones and apps → Add a phone. Choose the person, choose Yealink as the make, and enter the MAC address from the label on the back.
  2. Point the phone at Quaver Desk. Open the phone's web page (its IP address is under Menu → Status; a new phone's login is usually admin / admin). Go to Settings → Auto Provision and paste in the Server URL, User Name and Password from the Automatic set-up card in Quaver Desk. Press Auto Provision Now.
  3. Check it worked. The phone restarts its account and signs in. The phone log in Quaver Desk shows Phone downloaded its settings.

Setting up a whole office at once

Yealink phones read the provisioning address, with its login, from DHCP option 66 on your router. Add every phone in Quaver Desk first, set option 66 to the value on the Automatic set-up card, and plug the phones in. Factory-reset second-hand phones first, and remove option 66 (or keep it to a voice VLAN) once the rollout is done, because anything on that network can read it.

What the phone is given

  • Its account, encrypted: TLS for signalling and compulsory SRTP for audio.
  • The person's name on its screen, UK time with summer time, and *00 on its voicemail key.
  • A new random password for its own web page. The user and var logins get one too, so none keeps its default.
  • Busy lamp (BLF) keys for the colleagues you tick: see who is on a call, press to call them, press during a call to transfer.
  • A daily check for changes. Settings changed on the phone itself are put back at its next check.

Busy lamp keys on Yealink

Key 1 stays the phone's own line; colleagues follow on the next line keys in extension order, up to 20. Green or off is free, flashing is ringing, red is on a call.

Kept safe

  • A new phone has 24 hours to fetch its settings. The address it uses becomes its trusted address; a request from anywhere else waits for an admin to press Approve.
  • You can lock a phone to your office's internet address or host name, so it only signs in from there.
  • Every download, refusal and change is in the phone log.

The full walk-through, including DHCP option 66, approvals and troubleshooting, is in desk phone set-up.
