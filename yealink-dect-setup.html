Set up Yealink DECT cordless phones with Quaver Desk

Cordless phones for the warehouse, shop floor or reception. Quaver Desk sets up the Yealink W70B DECT base automatically, and its handsets either ring together on one extension or each get their own.

What is supported

The Yealink W70B base, with the handsets that register to it (such as the W73H, W78H, W59R, W57R and W56H), on current firmware.

Set up the base

  1. Add the base. In Quaver Desk go to Admin → Phones and apps → Add a phone. Choose the person, choose Yealink DECT base (W70B) as the make, and enter the MAC address from the label on the base.
  2. Point the base at Quaver Desk. Open the base's web page, go to Settings → Auto Provision, paste in the Server URL, User Name and Password from the Automatic set-up card, and press Auto Provision Now.
  3. Register the handsets to the base, as the handset's manual describes.

One extension, or one each

  • Shared. Out of the box, every handset on the base shares the base's extension and they ring together. For most small sites that is all you need.
  • One each. To give a handset its own extension, add it under Add a phone: choose its person and a name, and pick the base under Or a handset on a DECT base. It becomes the base's next handset (2, 3 and so on, up to 10 including the base's own). The base fetches the new settings straight away.

The base numbers handsets in the order they were registered to it: the first is handset 1, the second handset 2. The base's web page lists them, so you can match the people in Quaver Desk to the handsets you hand out.

Good to know

  • DECT handsets have no busy lamp keys.
  • A sign-in lock on the base covers all of its handsets.
  • Removing a handset frees its number for the next one added. Removing the base removes its handsets too.
  • Quaver Desk manages accounts 1 to 10 on the base: accounts typed into the base by hand are replaced.

Kept safe

  • A new phone has 24 hours to fetch its settings. The address it uses becomes its trusted address; a request from anywhere else waits for an admin to press Approve.
  • You can lock a phone to your office's internet address or host name, so it only signs in from there.
  • Every download, refusal and change is in the phone log.

The full walk-through, including DHCP option 66, approvals and troubleshooting, is in desk phone set-up.
