Integrations: single sign-on, Zapier, webhooks and an API

A phone system is more use when it talks to the rest of your tools. Quaver Desk signs people in with the accounts they already have, tells your other systems when calls happen, and lets your own software read its data.

Sign in with Microsoft or Google (Pro)

  • People sign in with their Microsoft 365 (Entra) or Google Workspace account. It is limited to your own directory or domain, and two-factor sign-in still applies.
  • Directory sync: Microsoft Entra, Okta and other identity providers can add people, keep their names and email addresses in step, and switch them off when they leave (SCIM 2.0). Nobody is deleted.

Webhooks, Zapier, Make and n8n (Plus and Pro)

  • Events for a call starting, a call ending, a new voicemail, a recording being ready and an AI summary being ready.
  • Each delivery is signed, so your system can check it came from yours, and retried if your end is down.
  • Works with Zapier, Make and n8n through their "catch a webhook" triggers: post missed calls to a chat channel, add a row to a spreadsheet, open a ticket.

An API for your own software (Plus and Pro)

  • Read people, phone numbers and call history, with tokens you make and revoke under Admin → API.
  • Click-to-call over the API (Pro): your CRM or line-of-business system rings a person's phones and dials the number for them when they pick up. Each calling token has its own limits, is UK-only unless you allow more, keeps an audit trail, and can be paused with one switch.

HubSpot CRM (Pro)

Calls logged against contacts with the AI summary and a recording link, caller names from HubSpot on your phone, and click to call. See HubSpot integration.

Desk phones

Yealink, Grandstream and Fanvil desk phones, and Yealink DECT bases, set themselves up from the web app, with busy lamp keys. See desk phone set-up.

Any SIP trunk

Quaver Desk isn't tied to one carrier. It works with Gamma, Voiceflex, Twilio, BT, Simwood and any other provider that offers a SIP trunk. See a phone system for your SIP trunk.
