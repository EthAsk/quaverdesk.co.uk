Features

Everything a small office needs from its phones

Built for teams of 2 to 100 people who want a proper phone system without a monthly bill per person.

Phone in the browser

A phone that runs in your browser, with call notifications, click-to-call and tel: links. Nothing to install on each computer.

Desk phones

Standard SIP desk phones (Yealink, Poly, Grandstream) ring alongside the browser. Always encrypted.

Transfer, hold and park

Transfer straight away or talk first, hold with your own music, and park calls on **1 to **9 for anyone to pick up.

Opening hours and menus

Each number follows its own hours. Send callers to a person, a ring group, a "press 1 for sales" menu or voicemail.

Ring groups and follow-me

Ring a team all at once or in turn. Calls can ring your own mobile too, with "press 1 to accept" so they never land in your personal voicemail.

Voicemail to email

Messages arrive in your inbox with the recording attached, and with a transcript and summary on Plus and Pro.

Call recording

Record by number with a spoken notice to callers. Recordings are encrypted on your server and deleted on your schedule. Emergency calls are never recorded.

AI with your own account

Transcripts and summaries of calls and voicemail, plus action items and caller sentiment on Pro. You connect your own AI provider, so you pay it directly.

Secure by default

Two-factor sign-in for admins, passkeys, encrypted phones, limits on outside calls to stop fraud, and 999 calls that are never blocked.

How it works

Up and running in an afternoon

Quaver Desk is a web app with its own FreeSWITCH voice server. You run both on one small Linux server or virtual machine, in your office or in the cloud.

Install it on your server

Follow the step-by-step guide on GitHub for Debian or Ubuntu. A virtual machine with 2 cores and 4 GB of memory comfortably runs a small office.

Connect your SIP trunk

Use the trunk wizard for Twilio, Gamma, BT, Simwood, Voiceflex or any other UK provider. Keep your numbers with them and pay only for the calls you make.

Add your team

Invite people by email, choose where each number rings, and you're taking calls. Add a licence key when you want Plus or Pro features.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I have to change my phone provider?

No. Quaver Desk works with any SIP trunk, so you can keep your current provider if they offer one, or choose a new one such as Twilio, Gamma, BT, Simwood or Voiceflex. Your numbers stay with your provider.

What do I need to run it?

A Linux server or virtual machine (Debian 12 or Ubuntu 24.04) that can be reached from the internet, a domain name, and a SIP trunk. The guide on GitHub covers the whole set-up, including the voice server, certificates and backups.

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. The Free plan needs no licence key and has no time limit. It makes and takes real calls for up to 3 people, so you can see how it works for you before upgrading.

What happens when my team grows?

When you add a person beyond your band, your admin pages offer to move you up to the next one, and the new key is applied straight away. You only pay the difference for the rest of the billing period.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your key keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for, then your system falls back to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted, and you can upgrade again at any time.

Can I see the code?

Yes. Quaver Desk is source-available under the Elastic License 2.0: you can read the code, run it for your own organisation and change it. You can't offer it to others as a hosted service. Paid features are unlocked with a licence key.

Who sees my calls and recordings?

If you run it yourself, only you: everything is stored on your server, with recordings and transcripts encrypted. If we host it for you, it is stored encrypted on our UK servers and we only use it to provide the service. Either way, if you turn on AI features, call audio goes to the AI provider you choose, using your own account.

Can I call 999?

Yes. Emergency calls are never blocked or recorded, and go out through your SIP trunk. But Quaver Desk depends on your server, internet connection and trunk provider, so it is not a replacement for a landline or mobile for emergencies. Make sure your provider has your correct address for caller location.
