Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This website is run by Ethan Askew trading as Quaver Desk, 119 Ridgehill Avenue, Intake, Sheffield, S12 2GN ("we").

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us

We use what you send us to reply and, if you buy from us, to provide your licence. We keep emails for as long as we need them for that and for our business records, and we don't share them except where the law requires.

If you buy a licence

Purchases are made at quaverdesk.app. We keep your business name, email address and order details to issue and renew your licence key. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card details. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Quaver Desk itself

Quaver Desk runs on your own server. Your calls, recordings, voicemail and contacts stay there, and none of it is sent to us. When your system checks its licence, it sends us only the licence key (and, when you move up a band, the band you chose).

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing sales@quaverdesk.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
