Call queues for small teams

When more people ring than you can answer, a queue keeps them in order. Callers hear hold music and their place in the queue, and the next free person gets the next call. Call queues are part of the Plus and Pro plans.

What callers hear

  • A greeting you write, read out when they join.
  • Hold music: your company's, or a different track for each queue.
  • Their place in the queue when they join, when it changes, and every minute.
  • After the longest wait you set, or straight away when nobody is logged in, they go to an overflow you choose: voicemail, a ring group, a menu or another queue.

Who the queue rings

Anyone already on a call is always skipped. For everyone else, each queue picks one of these:

  • Every free agent together. The first to pick up takes the call.
  • Longest idle first. Whoever finished their last call longest ago.
  • Round robin. The next person in turn.
  • Fewest calls today. Shares the work out evenly.
  • By priority (Pro). First choices ring before backups, so the specialists get the call when they're free and the wider team covers when they're not.

If the person rung doesn't answer, the caller carries on holding and the next round tries someone else.

For agents

  • Log in to and out of each queue from the home page, or dial *45 from any phone to log in to or out of them all.
  • Queue calls ring the phone in the browser and desk phones, like any other call.
  • Give a queue a short extension so colleagues can dial it or transfer a call into it.

For whoever runs the team

  • Live wallboard (Pro): callers waiting, the longest wait, who is free and today's answered and abandoned calls, on one page.
  • Queue reports (Pro): offered, answered, abandoned and overflowed calls, average and longest wait, and the share answered within your service level.
  • Listen in (Pro): chosen admins can listen to, whisper on or join a live call.

More on the wallboard and reports.

What it costs

Queues are included in Plus, from £15 a month for up to 5 users. Priority and backup agents, the wallboard and queue reports are in Pro, from £29 a month. Plans are priced by the size of your team, not per agent. See the price table.
