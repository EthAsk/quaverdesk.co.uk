Live wallboard, call reports and listening in

Know what your phones are doing, now and over time. Quaver Desk has a live wallboard for the office wall, reports you can read or have emailed, and a way for supervisors to listen in and help on a live call.

Live wallboard (Pro)

  • Calls in progress, each queue's waiting callers and longest wait, who is free and who is on a call, and today's totals.
  • Updates every few seconds, with a full-screen mode for a wall display.
  • Everyone can see it. Admins can hide callers' numbers from people who aren't admins.

Reports (Plus and Pro)

  • Calls by day and by hour: in, out, answered and missed, and time on the phone.
  • Download the calls in any date range as a CSV file for a spreadsheet.
  • Queue reports (Pro): offered, answered, abandoned and overflowed calls, average and longest wait, and service level.
  • A report per person (Pro): what each person handled.
  • By email (Pro): a weekly or monthly report sent to your admins.

Listen, whisper and join (Pro)

  • Chosen admins can listen to a live call, whisper to their colleague without the caller hearing, or join the call.
  • The owner confirms callers are told calls may be monitored before it can be switched on.
  • Emergency calls are never monitored, and every use is logged.

Call history and call logs

Every plan has call history for each person, and a call log for admins that says why a call went the way it did: which rule sent it where, and why a call was refused.

What it costs

Reports and CSV export are in Plus, from £15 a month for up to 5 users. The wallboard, queue and people reports and listening in are in Pro, from £29 a month. See the price table.
