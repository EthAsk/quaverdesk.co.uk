A FreePBX alternative that is ready to use

FreePBX is a capable and widely used way to run your own phone system. If what you want is a system for a small office that works out of the box, with a phone in the browser and no modules to choose, Quaver Desk is a different way to get there.

How Quaver Desk differs

  • One product, not a toolkit. The web app, the phone in the browser, voicemail to email, queues, recording and reports all come together and are set up from the same admin pages. There are no add-on modules to pick, buy or keep compatible.
  • Built for the people who use it. Admin pages are written in plain English for an office manager, not only for a telephony engineer. Each person gets their own page with their phone, voicemail and call history.
  • A phone in the browser as standard. Nobody has to install a softphone. Desk phones from Yealink, Grandstream and Fanvil set themselves up.
  • Modern sign-in. Two-factor sign-in and passkeys, and on Pro, sign in with Microsoft or Google and directory sync.
  • Safe defaults. Phones are always encrypted, outside calls have limits to stop toll fraud, and the trunk ports only accept your providers.
  • AI with your own account. Transcripts, summaries, search across calls and an AI receptionist, using your own AI provider.

What is the same

  • You run it on your own Linux server or virtual machine, and your calls and recordings stay there.
  • You bring your own SIP trunk from whichever provider you like: Gamma, Voiceflex, Twilio, BT, Simwood and others.
  • You can read the code.

Where FreePBX may suit you better

To be straight about it: FreePBX is open source and built on Asterisk, and if you want to write your own dial plan, need an unusual telephony set-up, or already have Asterisk skills in house, it gives you more room to build. Quaver Desk is source-available under the Elastic License 2.0 rather than open source, runs on FreeSWITCH, and is deliberately a finished product for small teams of 2 to 100 people.

What it costs

Up to 3 users is free, with no key and no time limit. Paid plans are priced by team size for the whole site: Plus from £15 a month and Pro from £29 a month for up to 5 users, with every feature of the plan included. See the price table.

Moving over

  1. Try it alongside. Install Quaver Desk on a new virtual machine and use the Free plan with a spare number.
  2. Re-create your call flow: numbers, opening hours, ring groups, menus and queues.
  3. Point your trunk at it and re-provision your desk phones.
