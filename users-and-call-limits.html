Users, lobby phones and call limits

Quaver Desk has three kinds of user. You can also stop anyone calling outside numbers, and turn each person's voicemail on or off. All of it is under Admin → Users.

Add a reception, lobby or hall phone

Use a desk-phone-only user for a phone that belongs to a place rather than a person.

  1. Under Admin → Users → Add a user, enter a name such as Reception and an email address, and choose the role Desk phones only (no sign-in). No invite email is sent, and the account can never sign in.
  2. Tick Colleagues only as well if the phone shouldn't call outside numbers.
  3. Under Phones and apps, add the phone for that user and set it up.

The user has an extension and can be in ring groups and have voicemail, like anyone else. Calls ring only their desk phones, because there is no softphone. Phones and apps shows whether each phone is online, with the address it signs in from and its model.

Switching between them

  • Make desk phones only signs the person out everywhere and ends their softphone, passkeys and two-factor. Their desk phones, ring groups and voicemail stay.
  • Make forward-only signs them out and also removes their desk phones and ring group places. Calls then ring their confirmed mobile or go to voicemail.
  • Give a sign-in emails them a link to set a password.

Moving a forward-only contact to any other type takes one of your plan's users.

Colleagues only: no outside calls

On Admin → Users, set someone's Calls to Colleagues only. Their phones, both desk phones and softphone, can then call any extension in the company but no phone numbers. A transfer to an outside number is refused too. Anyone who tries hears This phone can only call colleagues.

Use this to stop some people calling out. Use Stop outgoing calls to phone numbers on the Trunks page only to turn off outside calls for everyone.

999 and 112

999 and 112 always work, including from Colleagues only phones. If no trunk is set for outgoing calls, emergency calls still go out on any active trunk, starting with the one that carries most of your numbers. If no caller ID is set, they show one of your numbers. The Trunks page warns you when no trunk is active, because then 999 can't be made.

Check with your carrier how emergency calls and caller location work on your trunk.

Voicemail on or off

Each person's Voicemail can be switched on or off on Admin → Users. With it off, calls they don't answer just end. If nothing could ring, for example a forward-only contact with no confirmed mobile, the caller hears that they can't be reached. Their voicemail box and its messages are kept, so turning it back on restores everything.
