A phone system for your SIP trunk

A SIP trunk gives you numbers and calls, but no phone system. Quaver Desk is the phone system: connect your trunk from Gamma, Voiceflex, Twilio, BT, Simwood or any other provider, and your team can answer in the browser or on a desk phone, and calls can ring their mobile when they're out.

Works with your provider

The trunk wizard has settings for Gamma, Voiceflex, Twilio, BT and Simwood, and any other provider that offers a standard SIP trunk works too. Keep your numbers and your provider, and pay them only for the calls you make. Quaver Desk adds nothing per minute.

How it connects

  1. Add the trunk with the trunk wizard: choose your provider and enter the details they gave you.
  2. Point incoming calls at it. Quaver Desk shows the address to give your provider for the trunk.
  3. Add your numbers and choose where each one rings: a person, a ring group, a menu or voicemail, with its own opening hours.

What you get on top of the trunk

  • A phone in the browser and SIP desk phones.
  • Ring groups, menus, follow-me to mobiles, transfer, hold and park.
  • Voicemail to email, call recording, and AI transcripts and summaries.
  • Limits on outside calls to stop toll fraud, and 999 calls that are never blocked.
