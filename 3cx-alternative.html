Guide

A 3CX alternative for small UK businesses

If your phone system renewal has you looking around, Quaver Desk is worth a look. It's a complete phone system for teams of 2 to 100 people that you run on your own server, with your own SIP trunk, priced by the size of your team rather than per seat.

What you get

  • A phone in the browser that installs as an app, plus SIP desk phones (Yealink, Poly, Grandstream) and mobile apps such as Groundwire.
  • Opening hours per number, ring groups, "press 1 for sales" menus, follow-me to mobiles, and call park on **1 to **9.
  • Voicemail to email, call recording with a spoken notice, and AI transcripts and summaries using your own AI account.
  • Two-factor sign-in, passkeys, encrypted phones, and limits on outside calls to stop toll fraud.

What it costs

Up to 3 users is free, with no key and no time limit. Paid plans are priced in bands by team size: Plus starts at £15 a month and Pro at £29 a month for up to 5 users. No VAT is added. Calls are billed by your SIP trunk provider; Quaver Desk adds nothing per minute. See the full price table.

Moving over

  1. Keep your numbers. They stay with your SIP trunk provider. You point the trunk at Quaver Desk instead.
  2. Install it on a Debian 12 or Ubuntu 24.04 server or virtual machine. 2 cores and 4 GB of memory runs a small office.
  3. Re-create your call flow: numbers, opening hours, ring groups and menus.
  4. Re-provision your desk phones or switch people to the browser and mobile apps.

You can try all of it on the Free plan first, with real calls, before switching anything over.

Is it the right fit?

Quaver Desk is built for small offices that want a proper phone system without a monthly bill per person. If you'd rather not run a server, we can host it for you with UK calls included.
