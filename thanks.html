Pricing

Thank you

Your payment went through. Your licence key is on its way to the email address you used at checkout.

Adding your key

  1. Sign in to Quaver Desk as an admin.
  2. Go to Licence and paste the key.
  3. Your Plus or Pro features switch on straight away.

The key renews by itself each billing period: your system checks in once a day and picks up the new one.

Key not arrived?

It usually comes within a few minutes, but now and then it can take a few hours. Check your spam folder, then email sales@quaverdesk.co.uk and we'll sort it out.

You can change or cancel your subscription at any time from Manage your subscription.
