Bought a licence?

Your licence key is on its way to the email address you used at checkout.

  1. Sign in to Quaver Desk as an admin.
  2. Go to Licence and paste the key.
  3. Your Plus or Pro features switch on straight away.

The key renews by itself each billing period: your system checks in once a day and picks up the new one.

Key not arrived? It usually comes within a few minutes, but now and then it can take a few hours. Check your spam folder, then email sales@quaverdesk.co.uk and we'll sort it out.

Bought a hosted plan?

We're setting up your phone system now. An email with a link to set your password and sign in is on its way to the address you used at checkout, usually within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive, then email sales@quaverdesk.co.uk.

Calls beyond your included UK minutes, and international calls, are added to your next invoice at our published rates.

Changing or cancelling

You can change or cancel your subscription at any time from Manage your subscription.
