A self-hosted business phone system

Quaver Desk is a phone system you run yourself: a web app with its own FreeSWITCH voice server, on one small Linux server or virtual machine, in your office or in the cloud.

Why run your own

  • No per-minute markup. Calls go straight from your server to your SIP trunk, and you pay your provider directly.
  • Your data stays with you. Recordings, voicemail and call history are kept on your server, encrypted at rest. Nothing is sent to us.
  • One price per site. Licences are priced by team size in bands, so adding a person doesn't change the bill until you move up a band.
  • You can read the code. Quaver Desk is source-available under the Elastic License 2.0.

What you need

  • A Debian 12 or Ubuntu 24.04 server or virtual machine that can be reached from the internet (2 cores and 4 GB of memory is plenty for a small office).
  • A domain name for the phone system's address.
  • A SIP trunk from any provider, such as Twilio, Gamma, BT, Simwood or Voiceflex.

The install guide on GitHub covers the whole set-up, including the voice server, certificates and backups.

What you're responsible for

When you run it yourself, you keep the server updated, secure and backed up. If you'd rather not, we can host it for you, with updates, backups and 1,000 minutes of UK calls a month included.
