Step-by-step articles for admins and IT firms running Quaver Desk. The install and server guides are on GitHub.

Desk phones

  • Set up desk phones automatically
    Yealink, Grandstream and Fanvil: add the phone, point it at your provisioning address or use DHCP option 66, and check the phone log.
  • Busy lamp keys (BLF)
    See when colleagues are on a call or ringing, and call or transfer to them with one key.
  • Desk phone troubleshooting
    What each phone log message means, and what to check when a phone won't set itself up.

Users and calling

  • Add a reception, lobby or hall phone
    A desk-phone-only user: an extension and desk phone with no sign-in or softphone.
  • Stop someone calling outside numbers
    Colleagues only: extensions yes, phone numbers no. 999 and 112 always work.
  • Turn voicemail on or off for a person
    Off: unanswered calls end. Messages are kept for when it's back on.
