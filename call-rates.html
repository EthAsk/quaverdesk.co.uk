Call rates for hosted Quaver Desk

Your hosted plan includes a monthly allowance of minutes for UK calls. This page is what calls cost beyond that, and what calls to other countries cost, per minute. Rates are in pounds, and no VAT is added.

What your minutes cover

  • Included: incoming calls, and outgoing calls to UK landlines (01, 02, 03), 0800 numbers and UK mobiles. Calls between colleagues are always free and never counted. 999 and 112 are never charged or limited.
  • Past your minutes, UK calls carry on, up to as many minutes again as your plan includes, charged per minute: about 1.35p to landlines, 3p to mobiles and 0.7p for incoming calls. Mobile ranges, 03 numbers and special numbers vary a little, and are charged at their own rate from the table below.
  • Charged separately: international calls, and other UK numbers (070, 076, 084, 087 and 09) never use your minutes and are charged per minute, from the first second, at the price below.
  • Using your own SIP provider? Calls on your own lines use no minutes, and we charge nothing for them. Your provider bills you directly.

Price per minute, by country

Each call is rounded up to the next whole minute. Mobile is the usual price for mobile numbers; Highest is the dearest number type in that country, such as premium-rate and satellite numbers. Where a country isn't listed, we can't connect calls to it.

International calling is off until an administrator of your company allows each country, and premium-rate numbers stay blocked until allowed, so nothing here can be dialled by accident. Your company's page in the admin area shows the calls charged each month.
