Pricing

Partners

Resell Quaver Desk to your clients

For IT firms and MSPs who look after other businesses' phones. Sell your clients a proper phone system priced by team size, not per seat, and keep a healthy margin on every licence.

What partners get

  • Partner rates on every client licence, for as long as it runs. You see them in the partner portal once you're approved.
  • One partner portal for all your clients: add a licence in a minute, see each client's key, and download invoices.
  • Billed to you, monthly or yearly by card. You set your own price to your client.
  • Keys renew by themselves. Each client's install picks up its new key every billing period.
  • Any SIP trunk. Keep reselling the trunks you already sell, from Gamma, Voiceflex, Twilio or anyone else.
  • Hosted clients too. If a client would rather not have a server, talk to us about hosting them with us.

How it works

  1. Apply with a few details about your business.
  2. Sign the partner agreement we send you.
  3. Sign in to the partner portal and add your first client licence.
