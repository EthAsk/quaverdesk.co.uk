An AI receptionist for your phone line

Instead of "press 1 for sales", callers say what they need. The AI receptionist answers in conversation, puts them through to the option that fits, or takes a message. It is part of the Pro plan and uses your own AI provider account.

What it does

  • Answers in conversation. You write its instructions in plain English: who you are, what to ask, what never to say.
  • Puts callers through. You list where calls can go (a person, a ring group, a queue, a menu) and when to send them there. It never sends a call anywhere that isn't on your list.
  • Takes a message. When nobody can help, it writes down who called and why, and puts the message in a mailbox, which emails it to you.
  • Hands over. If it can't work out what the caller wants within a few turns, it puts them through to the fallback you chose rather than going round in circles.

Your AI account, your data

Quaver Desk doesn't resell AI. You connect your own account with an AI provider, so you pay the provider directly at their prices and their terms cover your callers' words. On a system you host yourself, the conversation goes from your server to your provider and nowhere else.

Where it fits

  • Out of hours, in place of a plain voicemail greeting.
  • In front of a small team, so the right person's phone rings first time.
  • As the overflow for a busy queue, to take a proper message.

Any phone number, menu option or queue overflow can point at a receptionist, and each number still follows its own opening hours.

The rest of the AI features

  • Transcripts and summaries of recorded calls and voicemails (Plus and Pro).
  • Action items and caller sentiment (Pro).
  • Search across what was said in calls and voicemails, or ask a question about them (Pro). People only search what they can already see.

What it costs

The AI receptionist is included in Pro, from £29 a month for up to 5 users, plus whatever your AI provider charges you for the calls it handles. See the price table.
